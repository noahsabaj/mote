# Mixture of experts for Mote — grilled and signed 2026-08-24

**Verdict (Noah, root option):** MoE goes into the pre-launch funnel as its own arm family. Not because it
is "the modern thing" but because of one property: **top-k experts add parameters without adding
activation memory**, and activation memory is what caps the flagship on the 8 GB card at 16384 context.
A bigger dense model cannot be afforded there (activations ∝ d_model); memory layers have the same
property but are ≥1B-scale literature with sparse-row optimisers and, on this box, PCIe 4.0 x8 rules out
host-resident rows (2608.21240 Fig. 1: 73–88 % of per-layer latency is PCIe on an RTX 4060).

## What MoE is for, in one paragraph
Decouple total parameters (memorised knowledge) from FLOPs per token. Memorisation tracks total params,
reasoning tracks active FLOPs and tokens per parameter (2508.18672). The price: memory for all experts, a
second router (balance, collapse, a lower optimal lr — 2502.05172 Finding 4: lr ∝ E^−0.25), and smaller
GEMMs that lose MFU on one GPU. The alternatives, ranked for a wall-clock-bound 8 GB box: memory layers /
product-key memory (2412.09764, 2601.00671) — better on facts, out of reach for the reasons above; looped
depth (MoR 2507.10524, MixerLoop 2608.18230) — the opposite trade, compute for params; bigger dense with
`--ckpt-main` — the law says a wash; Mixture-of-Depths — the H-Net's chunking already is byte-level MoD;
sparse upcycling of a trunk snapshot (2604.19835, 2502.03009, 2510.01185) — the fallback if the funnel
says no now; retrieval — Mote's search design covers the factual axis externally.

## Numbers behind the decision
Flagship = 95.9M params: 12 SwiGLUs 56.6M (59 %), Relation 28.3M, outer 10M. Trainer keeps fp32 masters
+ grads + Muon momentum = 12 B/param on top of the 4.31 GB peak (batch 1 × accum 4 @ 16384):

| layout | extra train memory | law's Δ at 7 days |
|---|---|---|
| E=4 / top-2 / half-size experts (d_ff 1024) | +0.68 GB | ≈ −0.03 nats (E=2-class capacity, granularity 2 on top) |
| E=8 / top-2 / half-size | +2.0 GB | ≈ −0.06 (E=4-class), serving gate 7.2 GB at the edge |

Joint MoE scaling law (Ludziejewski et al. 2502.05172, read in full; fit on 79M–2.7B-active Switch
Transformers, FineWeb-Edu, GPT-2 tokens, D/N ≤ ~25), Mote's numbers plugged in (ratio 5.75, 68.1 KB/s):

| flagship arm | tokens/param | E=2 | E=4 | E=8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 min | 0.2 | +0.070 | **+0.167** | +0.287 |
| 2 h | 0.9 | +0.027 | +0.065 | +0.112 |
| 8 h | 3.6 | 0.000 | +0.001 | +0.004 |
| 24 h | 10.7 | −0.014 | −0.031 | −0.050 |
| 7 days | 75 | −0.028 | −0.063 | −0.102 |

The advantage comes from a steeper data exponent (ν −0.197 → −0.219 at E=4), so **the funnel's 30-min / 2-h
arms would reject MoE even if the law is exactly right** — crossover at 3.7 tokens/param (8 h at flagship
preset; 3.4 h at 35M, whose crossover is D/N = 6). Memory-matched bigger dense (266M, fewer tokens) ties the
E=4 MoE on paper (3.026 vs 3.004) but does not fit at 16384; dense 190M + checkpointing is a wash (3.066 vs
3.067). The only byte-level MoE result, EntropyMoE 2608.06398 (1B, July 2026): −1.1 % BPB but 2.0× slower
wall-clock — at equal wall-clock it loses; our step overhead is the unknown. 4 epochs: the 7-day trunk is
41 GB over a 10 GB mix (Muennighoff 2305.16264: ≈3.7 unique-equivalent — fine; the day-10 mix-B extension
would be 6 epochs, where returns fade).

## The design (built, commit da14e1e)
`mote/model/moe.py` — `MoESwiGLU` in the FFN slot of every Relation block (layer 0 optionally dense):
experts as stacked tensors (`w1` [E, d, 2f], `w2` [E, f, d]) orthogonalised per slice in one batched
Newton–Schulz launch; router on AdamW in fp32; three exact-equal paths (dense masked for decode / CUDA
graphs / tiny inputs, per-expert loop on CPU, `grouped_mm` bf16 on CUDA); padded chunk rows leave the
statistics via the chunk mask threaded through `Isotropic`/`Block`. Routers: **lossfree** = DeepSeek-V3
bias balancing (bias buffer moved after each optimizer step, centred as in Moonlight) + sigmoid gates
renormalised over top-k × Moonlight's gate scale (2502.16982 Fig. 6, computed for our E/k: ≈1.3–1.4) +
sequence-level balance loss 1e-4 — the reference recipe Kakao 2608.20061 App. F keeps; **aux** = Switch
softmax + load-balance loss 1e-2 + z-loss 1e-3 (what the joint law was fit on). Telemetry per layer per
step: load, MaxVio, top-k mass; per-expert outputs reachable (`keep_expert_out`); eval logs expert usage
per layer; `mote.eval.moe_report` gives per-domain routing NMI / JSD (specialisation) on
`data/flagship_val`. FLOPs/MFU count k of E experts. Flags: `--moe E --moe-topk k --moe-ff f --moe-router
{lossfree,aux} --moe-dense-first --moe-aux-weight --moe-gamma --moe-gate-scale`.

## The funnel for MoE (signed)
1. **T1 smoke** (bug gate): `--preset smoke --moe 4` on the GPU, both routers, grouped path equality.
2. **T2 30-min flagship-preset arms** (KB/s, memory, router health, lr stability — never the loss):
   {E=4/top-2, E=8/top-2} × {lossfree, aux}, + layer-0-dense, + lr 5.7e-4 for the router that wins;
   loss-free wins ties (2608.20061 Fig. 11: the balancing family moved MaxVio, not loss, at their scale).
3. **T3-long 35M verdict, 7 runs × 12 h at equal wall-clock** (D/N ≈ 20, predicted −1.1 % ≈ −0.013 bpb
   for E=4-class, above the ±0.005 gate): dense at {lr0/2, lr0, 2·lr0}, each MoE layout at {lr0, lr0/2};
   constant LR (`--schedule trunk`) with `--eval-ema` so `mote.train.lr_horizon` can fit ln lr* vs ln D
   (2608.20061 §2.2.1) from the same runs and extrapolate to the trunk's 75 tokens/param. Noah sees the
   fit before any frozen number changes.
4. **T4′ flagship-preset 30-min confirm** of the winner's KB/s + memory, then the serving-beside-training
   bench on the MoE preset (gate 7.2 GB, first byte < 1 s, p95 ≤ 2× idle).
Queue slot: after JEPA round 2, before the confirm arm. New flags need a daemon restart at an arm boundary.

## The seven papers Noah sent (all read in full, 2026-08-24)
- **2608.20061** Kakao, μP + Muon LR transfer for MoE (155B/17B, 10T tokens). Two things that transfer:
  the optimal LR falls with token horizon (≈9.4e-4 at 0.5T → 3.85e-4 predicted at 10T; our sweep sat at 0.2
  tokens/param, the trunk runs at 75 — the freeze's own lr method cannot see this; measured by T3-long);
  the router recipe (bias balancing + seq-level loss, λ_routed retuned per E, layer-0 dense, keep bias
  updates live across the mix shift, MaxVio + per-domain MI/JSD telemetry). μP width transfer is validated
  only at fixed depth / fixed expert width — not our 35M → 96M step (6 → 12 layers).
- **2608.21236** RARE (steering MoEs): inference-time; routing is semantic not behavioural → per-domain
  expert-load telemetry is the right health readout; any activation steering on an MoE checkpoint must be
  projected onto the router's null space (patch, later).
- **2608.21240** SPICE (PCIe expert streaming): irrelevant (ours are resident); its Table 1 argues against
  low-rank residual experts (−2 to −4 pp), its Fig. 1 kills host-RAM memory layers on this box.
- **2608.17687** InnerExpert (hallucination detection from router signals): keep per-expert outputs
  reachable (done); never read router entropy as confidence.
- **2608.20761** Fuzzy-MoE (time series, dense soft mixture), **2608.18366** MAGMA (a GMM on an FPGA),
  **2608.18082** LongNovel (long-context faithfulness benchmark): nothing for the MoE plan; LongNovel's
  minimal-pair rater rule is an optional prefs-loop tweak.
