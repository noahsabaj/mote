# CoT for Mote — four-agent sweep, 2026-08-31

Four parallel sweeps (interleaved / adaptive / latent+small / adjacent), recency-weighted per the standing
rule; citation months skew Jun–Aug 2026 in all four (Aug ×25 across digests). Full digests in the session
record; this note keeps the load-bearing results and the design consequences. Nothing here re-opens signed
designs; the latent-feedback prereg gains one named amendment candidate (§6).

## 1. The paper Noah half-remembered

**"Interleaved Reasoning for LLMs via RL" — 2505.19640 (Apple + Duke, May 2025, v2 Jan 2026), not Kimi.**
Think/answer interleaving trained by RL with a **conditional intermediate reward** (gated on final-answer
correct + format + rising batch accuracy; ungated IR actively hurts: 0.392 vs 0.501). +12.5 % rel pass@1,
TTFT −80 %, response −37 %; the gap vs monolithic think-then-answer WIDENS with difficulty; works at 1.5B;
sub-answer ground truth optional (structure alone matches accuracy); a delayed-interleaving ablation shows
*timing* is the active ingredient (credit assignment). The Kimi association: **K2 Thinking (Nov 2025,
blog-only)** popularized think-between-tool-calls "interleaved thinking" as a model behaviour.

## 2. Convergent thesis A — allocation is computed, not emergent

- 2608.22347 (Aug, 4–6M audited architecture): value-shaped compute allocation does NOT emerge from
  gradients even with a stake sensor wired in (routing corr −0.01); an explicit allocator
  `argmax_n stake·p̂(success|n) − λn` over tiny fitted self-models captures the whole ceiling (corr +0.79).
  Self-agreement is near-worthless as a stop/selection signal (the model agrees with itself while wrong).
- 2607.20519 (Jul): learned halting gates in looped transformers entangle exit choice with loss weighting;
  fixed-prior depth supervision + plain entropy/margin readouts match or beat learned gates.
- 2608.07968 (Aug): budget rationing across questions does not emerge even in frontier models — it is trained
  or engineered.
- The two working learned forms, both ≤1.5B: **first-token mode routing** inside GRPO with hard per-mode
  caps, no std-normalization, asymmetric balance (2608.20256, Aug; collapses without difficulty spread in
  the mix — the Countdown negative) and **verifier-supervised stop-heads** over latent steps (SLPO
  2607.19691, Jul; works at GPT-2-124M; labels = "which k decoded correct", free from our verifiers).

**Mote:** the k-decider (k = 0..3 latent passes, think-block yes/no + budget) is an explicit small
allocator calibrated on the sim's ground-truth plan-length labels, optionally distilled into a first-byte
mode token later. Never gated on self-agreement. This is the no-knob adaptive-reasoning answer.

## 3. Convergent thesis B — exact verifiers are Mote's structural edge

- 2608.18931 (Aug, compute-normalized TTS): headroom captured ≈ verifier–truth correlation ρ_v. Open-ended
  ORMs sit at ρ_v ≈ 0.12 (selection near-random); PRM tree search destroys pool diversity. Fusion is the
  only consistent winner without a verifier. **The sim's ρ_v ≈ 1 is the one regime where sample-and-verify
  provably pays** — cheap k-sample + exact-check is a legitimate CoT rival at 96M.
- 2608.21871 LURE (Aug): pursuit-evasion self-play placing tasks on an explicit difficulty axis; GRPO's
  signal is provably maximal at p = 1/2 capture. The sim's hardness = plan length is literally the required
  interface — frontier placement replaces hand-tuned difficulty schedules for RLVR-1's sampler.
- Process ≫ outcome at small scale: 0.5B GRPO 33→64 % with per-step rewards vs 54 % outcome-only; thin
  process weight (0.1) is WORSE than none (2607.02869, Jul).

## 4. Convergent thesis C — hybrid latent + explicit, learned switch

- Both latent-CoT SFT families collapse to ~30 % of explicit-CoT accuracy on realistic ~350-token traces
  (2607.16972, Jul): latent = short-horizon accelerator; exact-token steps (arithmetic, tool args) are
  where compression dies. Answer-only-supervised latent planning depth ≈ 3 steps for tiny models (2604.06427).
- **TARPO 2606.05859 (Jun): per-token latent/explicit switching trained purely by RL** (2-way head on the
  hidden state, shared group advantage) beats both pure modes; pure-latent craters pass@32 — the routing
  stochasticity IS the exploration. Per-turn variant for agents: ALAR 2606.02871 (−44…−85 % tokens).
- RL on latent thoughts lands in weights, not decodable thoughts (2607.20952); genuine latent superposition
  appears ONLY in from-scratch-trained models (2604.06374) — Mote's regime.
- Existence proof at our size: BDH-CQ (2608.09888, Aug) — a 150M recurrent latent reasoner, ARC-AGI-1
  cost-accuracy Pareto SOTA.

## 5. The RL order at 96M, fixed by negative results

1. **SFT-CoT first, always**: the one sub-150M RLVR attempt (135M, 2606.22189) DECREASED accuracy —
   pass@k ≈ 0 leaves GRPO no group signal.
2. RLVR with: conditional intermediate rewards (2505.19640's gate); **length cost on successful episodes
   only** — a continuous penalty on failed rollouts structurally collapses (ACOER 2606.22716: 8/8 configs,
   even at 1/30 strength; all-fail groups diverge), while NO cost inflates length via GRPO's own credit
   loop (2606.17890); difficulty-spread task mix (checkable up front from our labels); zero-variance-group
   handling (VRPO-style expansion, 2608.01980).
3. Turn-level credit for think/act comes free from group statistics (HiDiffTIR 2608.21863; FACTOR
   2608.07118; TRACE 2607.13988 — no cold start) — gains LARGEST on the smallest models tested (4B).
4. Budget floor: a too-shallow think segment scores BELOW no-thinking (DeepTool 2605.29568 ablation) —
   budgets are tuned, not assumed. Compression has a monitorability price (2607.09786: decisive hints
   vanish 7–35 pp beyond length-matching) — the probes read these traces.

## 6. Direct hits on existing Mote lines

- **Latent-feedback prereg, amendment candidate:** T²MLR (2607.15178, Jul) — fusing the previous token's
  MIDDLE-layer state into an EARLIER layer beats top-state→input in matched-budget runs; our built fusion
  is top→input. A tap/injection-placement arm is cheap and the mechanism family is identical. MixerLoop
  (2608.18230, Aug; 15M/110M): loop the mixer only, FFN once — cheaper k>1 passes. Settling criterion
  (2608.18222, Aug): check per-pass hidden-state displacement before serving k above trained.
- **Test-time levers for a frozen 96M**: TTPO (2608.27448) with real labels instead of majority vote;
  test-time harness evolution (2607.08124) — the harness is the cheapest adaptive surface; ARTS
  (2606.21891) search-tree-into-weights via TTT.
- **Traps, flagged:** PRM tree search without an exact verifier; self-agreement anywhere; competitive
  self-play framing (the verifiable env does all the work, 2608.01559); any-order/diffusion reasoning
  (only ~Mote-scale non-CoT substrate, 2607.26504 at 125M — collides with the AR H-Net non-negotiables;
  parked as watched).
- Unread boundary pointers: SVR 2607.28457 (self-verification as compute controller), 2608.11233
  (recurrent-depth retrofit at 0.5B).

## 7. First-of-kind space

Nothing published: interleaved reasoning below 0.6B; RLVR-trained CoT below 0.5B (one failure at 135M);
byte-level / tokenizer-free CoT at any scale. Mote's CoT line would be the smallest interleaved-reasoning
data point by ~6× and the first byte-level one — with the sim's difficulty labels as an advantage no
web-data lab has.
