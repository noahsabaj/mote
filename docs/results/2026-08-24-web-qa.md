* After `nsweep_4` (a 30-minute screening arm, val 1.73) finished, the studio auto-served its checkpoint; the
  next chat answered 512 bytes of whitespace. That is the documented behaviour ("a finished job's final
  checkpoint becomes the served model") meeting a research queue: every arm that ends silently replaces the
  chat model.
* First byte during a job: 208 ms (35M arm) and 1.5 s (flagship arm) on a warm daemon; the one stall (>150 s)
  was the evaluation holding the gate (fixed above).

## Built 2026-08-25 (grilled in three rounds, signed "build it")

What each recommendation became; the two roots changed shape in the grilling.

* **R1 → root, pinned serving.** The served model is the pin (`.mote/config.json`); only a job started with
  `--serve` (`mote train start … --serve`, the Training sheet's *Put on the air*, `POST /api/training/serve`)
  answers chats with its EMA and pins its final checkpoint. Screening arms never swap the chat model. A manual
  load re-pins and takes the job off the air for the rest of its life (`unfollowed` on the load response; the
  sheet says so). `JobRecord.serve` survives resume copies; the EMA sync is skipped for every other job.
* **Serving device → root (Noah's question in round 2: "can we use the CPU for serving while a job runs?").**
  `Engine.moved(device)`: the engine lives on the CPU while any job runs or is about to (no arena, graphs, pool or
  GPU gate — training gets the whole card) and moves back to the GPU with one warm-up when the queue idles.
  Measured before signing: 35M 1.5 KB prefill 0.68 s then ~50 B/s; 96M ~45 B/s; a cold 4.6 KB read 10 s; the
  prefix store keeps continuations cheap. The trunk launch command gains `--serve`.
* **R2 → the pill and the Model sheet name what answers.** Pill: the run on the air with a live dot (the full
  sentence "Answering: the EMA of t3l_dense_8e-4 at step 12508 — on the CPU while training runs" is its label
  and tooltip), a `cpu` mark while serving is on the CPU; Model sheet: *Answering*, *Pinned*, and an *on the
  air* / *cpu* note with the numbers.
* **R3 → partial, server-told.** `{"type":"waiting","on":"prefill","bytes":N}` before a cold CPU read over
  1.5 KB, `{"on":"swap"}` when the engine lock is held; the reply shows the reason after 3 s
  (`Waiting — reading 4.6 KB of prompt on the CPU`). Unexplained stalls (a stuck kernel) still show the cursor.
* **R4** `(512 bytes, none printable)` under a blank finished reply. **R5** footer says *of drafts accepted*;
  the Bytes sheet row is *From the multi-byte head … (the footer's figure is the share of drafts accepted)*.
  **R6** theme is a three-item menu (System / Light / Dark, current one checked). **R7** `/pair` hides the
  link behind *show*. **R8** the Start form is gone — jobs start from the terminal; the sheet shows the phase
  beside *running* (`eval 3/16`, `checkpoint`), an *on the air* badge, and the on/off-air buttons.
  **R9** the phone key hint stays until the first message is sent from that browser (`hint.seen`).
  **R10** the switcher footer says *the newest 30 are kept*.
* Tests: `tests/test_jobs.py` (serve-gated sync, `set_serve`, phase), `tests/test_serve_release.py`
  (`moved`, waiting frames, the CPU never takes the gate), `tests/test_serve_pin.py` (pin round-trip, device
  follows the queue, only a job on the air replaces the served model, manual load unfollows, the endpoint),
  `tests/test_trainer_slices.py` (phase values). `svelte-check` 0 errors; `web/dist` rebuilt.
