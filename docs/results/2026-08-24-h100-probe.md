# 2026-08-24 — H100 probe, FlashAttention-4 read, and the kernel/compile decision

One ~18-minute H100 session (Lightning studio `vitruvian`, account A, interruptible; torch 2.13 cu126,
triton 3.7.1, driver 580, Mamba-3 + SSD Triton kernels loaded), then the design grilled and signed
(`docs/shape.md` § "Kernel and compile workstreams").

The H100 delivers 4.8× the local box from 22× the peak. Cost math at $3.82/h (interruptible list): the
7-day local trunk (41 GB of bytes) = 35 H100-hours ≈ 135 credits; one cooldown branch ≈ 27; RLVR-1 ≈ 20;
the remaining pre-launch queue ≈ 6. Decision: the flagship trains locally; the H100 is for experiments.

## Where the H100 step goes (profiled optimizer step = 8 micro-steps of 4×16384, 938 ms GPU)

* Mamba-3 kernels (upstream): `mamba3_siso_fwd` 80 ms + `_bwd_dqkv` 75 ms visible, ≈ 20–25 % with the rest.
* Casts / copies / elementwise: `aten::to` 58 ms, `copy_` 108 ms, `mul` 95 ms, elementwise ≈ **30 %**
  (5,193 `.to` and 6,488 `copy_` calls per step — the fp32-residual glue).
* Fused RMSNorm backward (`_layer_norm_bwd_kernel`, mamba_ssm): 88 ms = **9.4 %**, 245 µs per call on a
  [65k × 512] fp32 tensor ≈ 8× the bandwidth floor — the kernel is tuned for wide models.
* FlashRelation: Triton backward kernels 78 ms (8.3 %), forward below the 25-row cutoff (< 6 %), plus
  ~75 ms (8 %) of PyTorch glue inside its backward (row terms, casts). The full per-kernel table was lost:
  the rerun piped `profile_step` through `head -20`, which killed it before the trace export.
* Dense matmuls (`aten::mm`) 123 ms = 13 %.
* GPU-busy fraction: 72 % in the accum-8 run (164 ms wall vs 117 ms GPU per micro-step), 94 % in a shorter
  accum-2 rerun — the launch-bound gap is real but unresolved without the trace.

Estimates from these: a perfect Relation kernel buys ≤ 15 % of the H100 step; glue + norm backward + the
launch gap are the larger H100 levers (torch.compile / fusion); micro-batch 8 buys +55 % but is a 128 KB
step, not the frozen recipe.

## FlashAttention-4, verified 2026-08-24

* Paper (arXiv 2603.05451, 5 Mar 2026, MLSys 2026; read in full): Blackwell co-design — TMEM, 2-CTA MMA,
  fully asynchronous pipelines, tile 128×128; benchmarks are B200 only ("FlashAttention-3 does not run on
  B200"). Portable pieces: **conditional softmax rescaling** (rescale only when the row max grows by more
  than log 256; normalize by the true sum at the end), **partial software exp** (Cody-Waite + degree-3
  polynomial on FMA units for 10–25 % of entries; only where MUFU binds), **LPT block scheduling**
  (validated on FA3/H200: +4–8 % MHA).
* `flash_attn/cute` ("FA4" in the repo) has sm90 forward and backward kernels — FA3's design in CuTe DSL
  (~95 % of FA3 forward per Colfax, Nov 2025); head dim 96 supported (sm90 backward tile 64×128);
  `score_mod` / `score_mod_bwd` / `mask_mod` / `aux_tensors` are first-class. SM8x: forward only, no
  custom score_mod — never the 4060 Ti.
* FlexAttention `BACKEND="FLASH"` (PyTorch blog 4 Mar 2026; the file ships in our torch 2.13, needs
  `pip install flash-attn-4` and sm90+): arbitrary score_mod inlined into the CuTe kernels, backward
  auto-derived. H200 gains vs the Triton Flex: 1.3–1.9× forward, 1.4–2× backward. Limits: captured scalars
  are baked (λ must be an aux tensor), captured buffers cannot take gradients (dλ is computable outside:
  dA_i = (1−g_i)·dY_i·(Y_i−Ĩ_i), g_i = 1−exp(S_i−LSE_i)), backward score_mods that need the pre-softmax
  score (ours: dSiLU) "almost always spill registers".
* Relation's exact score function as a Flex score_mod (`where(q==kv, σ(u/τ), silu(u) − λ·log(q+1))`,
  causal incl. diagonal) is **correct** (max |err| 3.5e-3 vs the fp32 reference, identical to our kernel's)
  but on the 4060 Ti (Triton backend, contended by a running arm) **slower**: 3.16 ms fwd / 10.13 ms
  fwd+bwd vs our kernel's 1.92 / 8.68 ms at B1·H8·T2730·D96 bf16 (6.0 TFLOPS fwd, 4.6 fwd+bwd, FA
  convention). Our backward is 3.5× the forward (two passes, 7 tile-dots, the exp work twice).

## MUFU arithmetic (why FA4's exp trick matters on Hopper and not on Ada)

Per score element Relation needs sigmoid + exp in the forward (≈ 3 MUFU ops vs attention's 1) and ≈ 4
per backward pass (×2 passes today). H100: 4.2 T MUFU/s → a ~540 TFLOPS-equivalent forward ceiling and
~200 for today's backward, right where its measured ~19 % of peak sits. 4060 Ti: the MUFU:MMA ratio is ~8×
kinder; never binding. So the one-pass backward and CSE'd sigmoid matter on both GPUs; software exp only on
Hopper/Blackwell.

## Decision (signed 2026-08-24, details in docs/shape.md)

FlashRelation v2 at root depth (exact 64+32 head-dim tiles, one-pass backward with fp32 atomic dq and the
row terms in Triton, conditional rescaling, LPT order, exp2, per-device tile tables, two-pass kernels kept
as the deterministic fallback), then compile at root depth (custom ops, dynamic-T block compile,
static-shape routing/dechunk, CUDA graphs gated on memory). Both hot-swap into the trunk at a checkpoint
after a snapshot once exact + T1 smoke + ≥ 10 % faster. Rejected: Flex-FA4 (cloud-only, slower on Ada),
CuTe port, Blackwell choreography. Local flagship kernel share is an extrapolation (~30–40 %, from the
recorded 4 % Relation-backward share at the local preset × the 8× pairwise growth at 16384); the clean
profile runs when the local queue empties, and eager/`--compile` 10-min twins are queued after
`ab3_jepa_sig`.
